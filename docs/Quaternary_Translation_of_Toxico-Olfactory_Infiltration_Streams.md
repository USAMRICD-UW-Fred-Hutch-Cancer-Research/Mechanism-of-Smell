## **1\. The Quaternary Encoding Logic System (QELS)**

To translate continuous toxicological and olfactory fields into discrete digital strands, the physical boundaries of each volatile chemical vector are evaluated through a standardized 2-bit mapping routine \[VirusTC, 2026\]:

`[ Volatile Vapor Field ] ──► Extracted Boundary Constants (r, μ, α_avg)`  
                                          `│`  
                  `┌───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐`  
                  `▼                                               ▼`  
     `[ Bit 1: Spatial Radius (r) ]                 [ Bit 2: Net Polarizability (α) ]`  
       `- r < 3.5 Å  = 0                              - α < 4.0 Å³  = 0`  
       `- r ≥ 3.5 Å  = 1                              - α ≥ 4.0 Å³  = 1`  
                  `└───────────────────────┬───────────────────────┘`  
                                          `▼`  
                         `[ Base-4 Quaternary Translation ]`  
                           `- 00 = 0  |  01 = 1  |  10 = 2  |  11 = 3`

> * **Bit Position 1 (Spatial Field Length / Reach, \$r\$):**  
  * If \$r \< 3.5\\text{ \\AA}\$ (Compact gas configuration; fast diffusion) \$\\rightarrow\$ **0**  
  * If \$r \\ge 3.5\\text{ \\AA}\$ (Extended molecular cloud footprint) \$\\rightarrow\$ **1**  
> * **Bit Position 2 (Net Atomic Polarizability Volume, \$\\boldsymbol{\\alpha}\_{\\text{avg}}\$):**  
  * If \$\\boldsymbol{\\alpha}\_{\\text{avg}} \< 4.0\\text{ \\text{\\AA}}^3\$ (Rigid, low-mobility electron envelope) \$\\rightarrow\$ **0**  
  * If \$\\boldsymbol{\\alpha}\_{\\text{avg}} \\ge 4.0\\text{ \\text{\\AA}}^3\$ (Highly mobile, delocalized electron cloud) \$\\rightarrow\$ **1**

The parallel combination of these two binary states yields the assigned **Quaternary Base Code (0, 1, 2, or 3\)**, representing the unique biophysical field density signature of the incoming toxicant \[Sartori & Lloyd, 2014\].

## **3\. Electrochemical Conduction and Transmembrane String Parsing**

When a specific quaternary code packet—such as a **Base-3** signature from an invading phosgene field (\$\\text{COCl}\_2\$)—lops into the nasal mucosa, the olfactory sensory neuron (OSN) parses this digital digit back into an acute electrochemical impulse \[Sartori & Lloyd, 2014; VirusTC, 2026\].

The high torque forces (\$\\boldsymbol{\\tau} \= \\boldsymbol{\\mu} \\times \\mathbf{E}\$) lock the toxic molecule into the matching post-synaptic receptor pocket, forcing a conformational change that initiates an intracellular signaling cascade:

> 1. **Cyclic Adenosine Monophosphate (cAMP) Influx:** The internal G-protein pathway (\$\\text{G}\_{\\text{olf}}\$) fires, activating adenylyl cyclase (ACIII) to synthesize cAMP from intracellular ATP pools.  
> 2. **Ion Channel Gating:** High local cAMP levels open cyclic nucleotide-gated (CNG) channels, causing an immediate inward current of sodium (\$\\text{Na}^+\$) and calcium (\$\\text{Ca}^{2+}\$) vectors from the mucous layer.  
> 3. **Chloride Outflux Acceleration:** The localized calcium influx opens ANO2 chloride channels, rapidly driving native chloride ions (\$\\text{Cl}^-\$) out of the cell dendrite.  
> 4. **Action Potential Wave Conduction:** This parallel ion migration shifts the intracellular charge density, forcing the transmembrane voltage past the depolarization limit (\$\\Delta V\_{\\text{mem}} \\ge \-40\\text{ mV}\$) \[VirusTC, 2026\]. The resulting high-frequency electrical spike travels through the perforations of the low-permittivity cribriform plate (\$\\varepsilon\_r \= 11.40\$) and straight into the olfactory bulb glomeruli for real-time cortical mapping and emergency triage response \[Gabriel, 1996; VirusTC, 2026\].
